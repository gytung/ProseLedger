Source format & limits

Supply 2–10 notes, 80–4,000 characters per note and at most 24,000 in total. Start each with [S1] Title, followed by its content, then [S2] Title for the next source. Only these notes are used as evidence; this page does not search the web. Import TXT or Markdown; PDF upload is not supported. Long notes are rejected, never silently shortened.

Start with 2 calls, read the draft, then compare revision routes from that same draft. Full comparison uses up to 14 calls and usually takes several minutes; time depends on your provider.

What does “more natural” mean here? Explore the six editing rules.

Natural academic writing makes the argument easier to follow without changing its scientific meaning. Each rule has an applicability condition and an exception. These are editorial specifications to test, not proof of improved writing.

Teaching examples only. They are not results from your run and provide no facts for your proposal. Hyphens, technical terms, passive voice and “we” are not automatically errors.
